# Hands On — demo video script (< 3 min)

Record the **Lens Studio Interactive Preview** (Spectacles device). Tick `runDemo` off — drive it
live with the mouse (click = pinch) so it feels like a person, or tick `runDemo` on and let the
scripted walkthrough run while you narrate. `kneelCamera` should stay ON so the chest is in frame
from the first second. Record at 1080p; the preview window fills the frame.

Total target: **~2:40**. Speak calm and unhurried — that contrast (calm voice, scary situation)
is the whole point of the product.

---

## 0:00–0:15 — The hook (voiceover over the idle scene)

**On screen:** the kneeling view over the mannequin, the "Someone collapsed?" orb floating and bobbing.

> "When someone's heart stops, the person next to them is usually not a medic. Their hands are on
> the chest, their eyes are on the patient — a phone can't help. This is Hands On: a CPR and AED
> coach for Snap's SPECS, drawn onto the person in front of you."

## 0:15–0:35 — Start the flow

**On screen:** pinch the orb → call card appears ("Call 112/911 on speaker. Shout for an AED.") →
press **Done** → pinch the mannequin's chest → the hand target snaps onto the lower breastbone →
press **Start**.

> "One pinch starts the coach. It tells you to call emergency services and shout for an AED —
> then you pinch the chest once, and everything anchors there, at real size, where the work is."

## 0:35–1:10 — The pacer (hero shot)

**On screen:** the ring pulsing at 110/min on the chest, counter looping 1–30, timer running,
PUSH/RISE bar bouncing beside the hand target. Hold this shot ~30 s. Mention the click is audible.

> "The ring pulses at 110 beats a minute with a click, right on the breastbone. The counter keeps
> your sets of thirty, and the push-rise bar teaches full recoil — most people's compressions are
> too shallow because they never let the chest come back up."

## 1:10–1:25 — Rescuer swap

**On screen:** press **2 min** (skip) → chime, amber "Switch rescuers now" cue, ring turns amber,
then clears itself.

> "At two minutes it chimes for a rescuer swap — compressions weaken long before you feel tired —
> and the cue clears itself so nothing clutters your view."

## 1:25–1:55 — AED pads (the memorable interaction)

**On screen:** press **AED here** → tray with two pads appears, glowing numbered outlines on the
body. Grab pad 1, drop it on the **wrong** outline → it bounces back with a hint. Drag both pads
onto their outlines → "Stand clear. Don't touch." → analysing countdown → **SHOCK** appears.

> "When the AED arrives, you grab each pad and drag it onto its glowing outline — wrong spot, it
> bounces back. Both pads on, and it tells you to stand clear while it analyses."

## 1:55–2:10 — Shock + re-analysis loop

**On screen:** press **SHOCK** → flash + zap, pacer resumes instantly. Then press **2 min** again →
with pads still on, the Lens pauses for **analysing** again instead of a swap cue.

> "After the shock, compressions resume immediately — hands-off time is what kills. And every two
> minutes, just like a real AED, it re-analyses the rhythm and coaches the next shock."

## 2:10–2:30 — Handover summary

**On screen:** press **Help arrived** → summary card: total time, **first push at**, cycles,
shocks, hands-off time, **longest pause**, compression fraction, and the timestamped event timeline.

> "When paramedics arrive, it hands over exactly what they ask for: how long the patient was down,
> time to the first compression, shocks given, hands-off time and compression fraction —
> nobody has to remember anything under stress."

## 2:30–2:40 — Close

**On screen:** press **Start again** → everything resets to the idle orb. Freeze on the orb.

> "It never claims to detect the patient — it coaches, paces and choreographs. Hands On: hands on
> the chest, eyes on the patient, the coach in your glasses."
